## Ontology

- **LiDAR scans** represent acquisitions or coherent campaigns. Atlas footprints remain separate geometry linked through `atlas_survey_ids`.
- **Investigations** represent excavation, survey, coring, laboratory sampling, community research, and other evidence-producing activity.
- **Expeditions** represent historically documented journeys used to organize route-specific evidence.
- **Route locations** connect a journey to public modern geographic anchors or explicitly approximate historical areas. They never substitute for protected archaeological-site coordinates.
- **Archaeological sites** represent only named archaeological entities—not corridors, regional centroids, or survey zones.
- **Papers** document or analyze scans and investigations.
- **Organizations** connect to scans and investigations as operators, analysts, funders, repositories, or data custodians when the sources support that role.

Exact archaeological coordinates are omitted or generalized. Generated Paper notes distinguish bibliography discovery from source acquisition and source review.
