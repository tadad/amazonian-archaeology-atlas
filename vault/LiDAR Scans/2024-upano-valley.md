# Upano Valley urban network

In the forested Upper Amazon drainage at the eastern foot of the Andes, LiDAR exposed a connected landscape of platforms, plazas, roads, drainage works, and agricultural terraces.

## Reported metrics

- 600 km² acquired
- 300 km² intensively analyzed
- >6,000 platforms and 15 urban centers
