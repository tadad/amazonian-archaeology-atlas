# EBA–Sustainable Landscapes released transects

The public EBA archive exposes the actual distributed acquisition footprints behind a large part of Brazil’s ecological LiDAR coverage. Some of these data were later repurposed for archaeology, but the archive itself is not an archaeological screen.

## Reported metrics

- 901 released acquisition records
- 1,002 polygon parts
- 2016 and 2017–2018 campaigns
- Public vector footprints and point-cloud archive

## Geometry

The atlas maps 1 linked acquisition record through the separate footprint layer; the graph record does not duplicate protected or source-derived geometry.
