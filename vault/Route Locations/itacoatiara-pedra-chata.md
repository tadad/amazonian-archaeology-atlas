# Itacoatiara / Pedra Chata research area

## Why it is on this map

Itacoatiara is a major modern settlement on the descent corridor and a public anchor for lower-Urubu archaeological research.

## Archaeology

The Pedra Chata ceramic study reports a dated context around 200–50 BC and a possible cultural sequence continuing into the beginning of the colonial period ([[Papers/2017-gama-pedra-chata-itacoatiara|Gama 2017]]). That long record supports investigation of continuity and change around 1542 but does not identify Pedra Chata as a place described by the expedition.

## Caution

The point is the modern city, not the protected archaeological site.
