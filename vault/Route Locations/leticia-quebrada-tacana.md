# Leticia / Quebrada Tacana

## Why it is on this map

Leticia is a modern route city and a public anchor for archaeological research near the Peru–Colombia–Brazil frontier. It fills part of the long gap between the lower Napo and São Paulo de Olivença without presenting a modern city as an expedition stop.

## Archaeology

Quebrada Tacana is an archaeological-dark-earth site in Leticia County. Costa and colleagues report a ceramic-bearing anthropogenic horizon, an area of about two hectares, and charcoal dated to approximately 1270–1060 years BP ([[Papers/2011-costa-et-al-quebrada-tacana|Costa et al. 2011]]).

## Caution

The marker is the modern city center. It neither publishes the archaeological-site coordinate nor identifies Quebrada Tacana with a named settlement in Carvajal.
