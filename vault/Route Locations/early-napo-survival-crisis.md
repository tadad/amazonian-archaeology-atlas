# Early Napo survival crisis

## Expedition event

Soon after the separation, the barque struck floating timber, stove a plank, and had to be beached and repaired. The narrative then describes days without settlements or provisions, eating leather and herbs, and the decision to continue downstream because a return against the current seemed impossible ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 102|PDF p. 102]]). It later reports that seven companions had died from hunger before the party left the first sustained provisioning stop ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 104|PDF p. 104]]).

## Caution

This is an event-sequence marker, not a claimed camp. Its position is deliberately broad and follows the modern Napo only to make the order of events legible.
