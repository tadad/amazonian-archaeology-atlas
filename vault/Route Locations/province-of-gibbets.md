# Province of the Gibbets report

## Expedition event

The narrative describes seven structures displaying human heads, roads said to be paved and lined with fruit trees, a food seizure followed by fighting, and the death of a local leader ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 111|PDF p. 111]]). A captive woman subsequently relayed a story about white people and two white women inland; that statement is hearsay within the account, not an observation ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 112|PDF p. 112]]).

## Caution

Only the event's position in the narrative sequence is reasonably constrained. The marker must not be read as an identification of the reported roads, heads, or inland people with a specific modern place.
