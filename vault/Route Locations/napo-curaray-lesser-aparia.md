# Napo–Curaray / Lesser Aparia

## Expedition event

The narrative says that after leaving the first sustained provisioning settlement the vessels narrowly survived turbulent meeting waters at a large right-bank tributary, then crossed a long uninhabited reach ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 104|PDF p. 104]]). This marker does not assert that the Curaray was that exact tributary.

## Reported polity

Recent scholarship places the center of the reported Lesser Aparia, associated with the lord Irimara, near the Curaray confluence ([[Papers/2025-cabrero-aparia#Page 1|Cabrero 2025, PDF p. 1]]). The broader itinerary reconstruction extends the relevant corridor through much of the middle and lower Napo ([[Papers/2019-arroyo-kalin-rivas-napo#Page 5|2019, PDF p. 5]]).

## Caution

This marker is approximate because the historical identification is the uncertain part—not because the modern rivers are unknown. Phase Napo archaeology offers a research program for the corridor, not a verified palace, capital, or named settlement.
