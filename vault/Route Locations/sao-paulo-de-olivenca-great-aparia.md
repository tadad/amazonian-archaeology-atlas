# São Paulo de Olivença / Great Aparia

## Expedition event

The expedition narrative names Aparia as a principal lord, records discussion of numerous other rulers, and describes building a second brigantine during a 35-day stay, using cotton and pitch for caulking ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 105|PDF p. 105]]). The account says the expedition remained through Lent and departed on April 4 ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 106|PDF p. 106]]).

## Modern hypothesis

Cabrero proposes modern São Paulo de Olivença and its surroundings as the principal Aparia center or zone of influence. The paper explicitly states that no conclusive evidence presently fixes the capital, so the atlas shows an approximate area rather than a known point ([[Papers/2025-cabrero-aparia#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
