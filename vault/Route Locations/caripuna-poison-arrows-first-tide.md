# Caripuna reports, poison arrows, and first tide

## Expedition event

A translated captive's account describes women rulers, stone and walled cities, and sun houses plated with gold; the editor explicitly warns that the vocabulary and translation cannot be affirmed ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 115|PDF p. 115]]). In the following sequence, the expedition meets poisoned arrows, loses Antonio de Carranca, and first notices the ebb tide. The same page contains Carvajal's marvelous story of a bird that allegedly followed the vessels and called out near settlements ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 116|PDF p. 116]]).

## Caution

The lower-Amazon placement reflects narrative order and the onset of tidal observations. It does not locate the reported cities, sun houses, or bird story; those remain claims attached to this broad episode.
