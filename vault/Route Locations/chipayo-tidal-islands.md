# Chipayo and tidal-island fighting

## Expedition event

The narrative describes inhabited islands, strong tides, an arm of the sea, and repeated fighting with piraguas. García de Soria is said to have died from a poisoned-arrow wound, after which the vessels entered the territory called Chipayo in the edition ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 116|PDF p. 116]]).

## Caution

The marker uses the modern Gurupá corridor only as a broad tidal-channel anchor. It is not a proposed coordinate for Chipayo or any particular encounter.
