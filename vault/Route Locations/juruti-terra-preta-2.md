# Juruti / Terra Preta 2 research area

## Why it is on this map

Juruti is the western limit used in one reconstruction of the extensive São João province ([[Papers/2020-lima-moraes-sa-tapajonico#Page 2|Lima, Moraes, and Sá 2020, PDF p. 2]]) and has a concrete municipal archaeological record.

## Expedition event

The narrative describes closely spaced villages, another ambush, a large wound to Carvajal's eye, and a province it calls São João extending for more than 150 leagues ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 114|PDF p. 114]]). Juruti is a modern research anchor near the western edge of one scholarly reconstruction, not the coordinate of any reported village.

## Archaeology

The Terra Preta 2 study used 46 probes and soil profiles to investigate a roughly 28-hectare anthropogenic-dark-earth site. Ceramic distributions and soil stratigraphy indicate spatially varied activities during occupation ([[Papers/2020-costa-et-al-terra-preta-2-juruti|Costa et al. 2020]]).

## Caution

The point is modern Juruti, not Terra Preta 2. The archaeological extent does not by itself establish the political boundaries described in later reconstructions.
