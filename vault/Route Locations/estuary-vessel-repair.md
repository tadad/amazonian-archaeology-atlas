# Estuary vessel damage and final repairs

## Expedition event

One brigantine struck a snag and flooded while the party faced another large attack. The account then describes eighteen days of vessel repairs and nailmaking ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 117|PDF p. 117]]). Before entering the sea, the expedition reportedly made rigging from grass and sails from blankets, survived for two weeks on shellfish, and departed without a pilot or compass ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 118|PDF p. 118]]).

## Caution

This marker represents a cluster of late-estuary events rather than one camp. Channels, islands, and shorelines have changed, and the narrative does not provide a surveyable location.
