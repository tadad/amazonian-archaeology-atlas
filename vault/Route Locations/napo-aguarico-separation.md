# Napo–Aguarico confluence

## Why it is on this map

Route scholarship commonly places the agreement and separation near this confluence. The point marks the requested start of Orellana's independent descent, not the expedition's earlier overland route from Quito.

## Report and evidence

The reviewed narrative begins after the separation, explains that the current and distance prevented a return, and follows the party downstream toward the ocean ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 102|PDF p. 102]]). The text does not provide a surveyable camp coordinate, so only the modern confluence is treated as located.
