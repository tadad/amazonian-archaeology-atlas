# Parintins / Tupinambarana and Macurany

## Why it is on this map

Parintins is a major modern route city with two complementary archaeological records: an urban ceramic collection from Orla de Parintins and a mapped anthropogenic landscape at nearby Macurany.

## Expedition event

The expedition's warrior-women battle is mapped separately and very approximately in the lower Nhamundá/Trombetas corridor. Parintins is retained as the primary research node in this neighboring stretch; the narrative says women fought at the head of the force and reports that Carvajal was wounded, while the editor leaves the story's credibility open ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 113|PDF p. 113]]).

## Archaeology

Macurany preserves earthen wharves, middens, terra preta, and managed forests interpreted as evidence for permanent habitation, infrastructure, and a possible pre-contact port ([[Papers/2024-ellis-et-al-macurany|Ellis et al. 2024]]). The Orla de Parintins dissertation documents Pocó-Açutuba ceramics and a longer, culturally diverse history of occupation on Tupinambarana Island ([[Papers/2023-machado-tupinambarana|Machado 2023]]).

## Caution

Neither study proves that Parintins or Macurany was a specific named settlement in the expedition narrative. The marker is the modern city and does not refine either site location.
