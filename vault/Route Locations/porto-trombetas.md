# Porto Trombetas

## Archaeology

Porto Trombetas anchors a substantial modern research record of Pocó and Konduri ceramic occupations in riverine and interfluvial settings. Guapindaia and Lopes synthesize both earlier and recent investigations, including the Boa Vista work ([[Papers/2012-guapindaia-lopes-porto-trombetas#Page 15|PDF p. 15]]).

## Expedition event

The research area lies within scholarly reconstructions of the densely settled lower-Amazon São João province. Markham's editorial note places the warrior-women encounter near the Trombetas mouth ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 113|PDF p. 113]]), but the event remains a separate approximate marker so this node stays centered on the well-documented archaeology.
