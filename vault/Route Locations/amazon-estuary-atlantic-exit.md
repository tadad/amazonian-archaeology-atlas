# Amazon estuary / Atlantic exit

## Why it is on this map

The point completes the requested river route. The narrative describes leaving the river between estuarine islands and entering the Atlantic ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 118|PDF p. 118]]).

## Caution

This is deliberately a very broad marker. It does not claim one exact 1542 channel through a dynamic estuary, and it carries no implied archaeological-site location.
