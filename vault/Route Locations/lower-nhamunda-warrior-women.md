# Lower Nhamundá / warrior-women report

## Expedition event

The narrative says that women fought at the head of an Indigenous force. The reviewed edition itself leaves the story's credibility open, and Markham's note places the encounter near the Trombetas mouth ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 113|PDF p. 113]]).

## Why this point is retained

Unlike placeless El Dorado-style hearsay, the report has a defensible lower-Amazon regional setting. Guapindaia and Lopes connect the warrior-women tradition with the upper Nhamundá while placing the wider São João province through the Nhamundá–Trombetas–Tapajós corridor ([[Papers/2012-guapindaia-lopes-porto-trombetas#Page 6|PDF p. 6]]). The encounter remains uncorroborated; the region's Pocó and Konduri archaeology is real but does not prove the story.
