# Angoteros and the middle Napo

## Why it is on this map

Angoteros is a modern, mappable community in the middle-Napo corridor that scholarship associates with the broader area called Lesser Aparia in reconstructions of Carvajal's itinerary.

## Expedition event

In this broad reach of the route, the narrative first describes obtaining provisions at a defended settlement and then deciding to build another brigantine. It reports feather ornaments, gold ornaments worn by leaders, and information about powerful inland rulers ([[Papers/1859-markham-orellana-voyage#Page 103|PDF p. 103]]). The modern Angoteros anchor does not identify that settlement; it only keeps the event beside the relevant middle-Napo archaeology.

## Archaeology

Survey work reports Phase Napo ceramics at Angoteros Pampa and related evidence elsewhere along the river ([[Papers/2019-arroyo-kalin-rivas-napo#Page 11|PDF p. 11]]). The authors say this archaeology is relevant to the Lesser Aparia question but does **not** directly prove that a particular investigated site was a settlement named by the expedition ([[Papers/2019-arroyo-kalin-rivas-napo#Page 17|PDF p. 17]]).
