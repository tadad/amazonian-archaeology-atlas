# Orellana's descent of the Amazon

This atlas begins where Francisco de Orellana's party separated from Gonzalo Pizarro near the Napo–Aguarico confluence, then follows the Napo and Amazon/Solimões river corridor to the Atlantic. It does not map the earlier overland departure from Quito.

The dashed route is intentionally schematic. The map points distinguish known modern geographic anchors from approximate historical identifications, and the linked records keep reported cities, rulers, dense settlement, and the women-warriors account separate from modern archaeological evidence.

The date range follows the conventional 1541–1542 expedition chronology. Editions of the narrative differ in printed dates and spellings, so page-specific claims cite the edition actually reviewed.
