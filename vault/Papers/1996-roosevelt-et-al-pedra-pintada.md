# Paleoindian Cave Dwellers in the Amazon

## Notes

Foundational report on stratified late-Pleistocene occupation at Caverna da Pedra Pintada near Monte Alegre. The study uses radiocarbon and luminescence dating alongside lithic, botanical, faunal, and rock-art evidence.

The occupation is far earlier than Orellana's journey. Its value to this atlas is as a major archaeological research anchor in a route corridor that also contains much later ceramic and rock-art occupations.
