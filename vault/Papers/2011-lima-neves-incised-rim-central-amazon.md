# Incised Rim/Barrancoid Ceramics in the Central Amazon

## Notes

Ceramic chronology for the Negro–Solimões confluence based on Central Amazon Project research. The article reassesses the relationships among the Açutuba, Manacapuru, and Paredão phases rather than treating them as isolated typological units.

## Atlas connection

Linked to [[Route Locations/rio-negro-confluence|Rio Negro confluence]], a public geographic anchor for the central-Amazon research area.
