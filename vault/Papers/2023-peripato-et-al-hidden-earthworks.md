# More than 10,000 Pre-Columbian Earthworks Are Still Hidden throughout Amazonia

## Notes

Amazon-wide LiDAR survey and predictive model reporting 24 previously undocumented earthworks, including ten provisional Acre records (ACE-01 through ACE-10) between Rio Branco and Senador Guiomard.
