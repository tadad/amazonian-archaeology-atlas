# Archeometric study of pottery shards from Conjunto Vilas and São João

## Notes

Laboratory characterization of ceramic fragments from the Conjunto Vilas and São João sites in the Middle Solimões macro-region. Optical microscopy, FTIR, XRF, XRD, and SEM-EDX identify mineral phases and tempers including cauixi and caraipé.

## Page 1

The paper places the assemblages within the Pocó, Tefé, and Caiambé occupation phases and connects the material to long-duration settlement research around modern Tefé.
