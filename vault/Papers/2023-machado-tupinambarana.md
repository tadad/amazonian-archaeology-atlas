# Os antigos habitantes da Ilha de Tupinambarana

## Notes

Dissertation analyzing ceramics from the urban Orla de Parintins archaeological site. The study investigates Pocó-Açutuba materials, the antiquity and cultural diversity of occupation on Tupinambarana Island, and the archaeological historiography of Parintins.

Linked to [[Route Locations/parintins-macurany|Parintins / Tupinambarana]]. The public marker generalizes both this urban collection context and nearby Macurany research.
