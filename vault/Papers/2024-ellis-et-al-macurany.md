# A Port by Any Other Name

## Notes

Spatial and landscape study of Macurany near Parintins. Survey and topographic mapping documented earthen wharves, middens, terra preta, and cultural forests around Lake Parananema.

## Pages 128–148

The authors interpret the combined features as evidence for permanent dense habitation, landscape engineering, and a possible pre-contact port or exchange node. They distinguish this interpretation from a claim that Macurany was necessarily the center of a centralized polity.
