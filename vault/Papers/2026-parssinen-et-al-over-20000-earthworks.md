# Over 20,000 Precolonial Earthworks in the Southwest Amazonia

## Notes

Open-access Nature article using 2024 airborne LiDAR to document 432 earthworks, of which 396 were previously unknown, refine the proposed Aquiry extent, and model the regional earthwork count and population.

The ten narrow survey lines covered 4,497 km². Six systematic Rio Branco–Lábrea transects yielded 347 structures: 320 toward the Rio Branco end and 27 toward the lower-density Lábrea end. The long northern Purus–Juruá upland lines produced no detections beyond the previously known Carauari cluster; two finds near Lábrea are in contemporary Indigenous territory and are intentionally not located.

Supplementary Table 2 publishes decimal-degree coordinates for 406 of the 432 campaign detections. The atlas generalizes those public records to occupied 0.01-degree cells and does not reproduce exact coordinates or site names. The 26 records omitted by the authors because they fall in contemporary Indigenous territories remain unmapped.
