# Os discos perfurados do período Tapajônico

## Notes

Technological analysis of Tapajonic perforated discs and their archaeological contexts in Santarém, with a concise ethnohistorical geography of the lower Amazon.

## Page 2

The paper locates Carvajal's populous São João province between modern Juruti and Prainha and identifies the area of modern Santarém as its proposed sixteenth-century political center. It then grounds that discussion in excavated Tapajonic material, workshops, and exchange networks.

## Page 3

The authors discuss the strategic position of the Tapajós–Amazon confluence and the broader circulation of Santarém material culture.

## Page 7

The technological study documents production choices and contexts for perforated discs, supplying modern archaeological evidence independent of the expedition's more spectacular reports.
