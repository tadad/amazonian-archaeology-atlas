# Entre a história antiga amazônica e seu presente etnográfico

## Notes

Long-duration synthesis joining archaeology, colonial documents, and present Indigenous histories in the Middle Solimões. Historical province names are treated as analytical geography rather than fixed ethnic labels.

## Page 8

Following Porro, the paper places Machiparo from around the Japurá mouth to before the Coari mouth and Yaguamales between the Coari and Purus mouths. The same section connects these historical geographies to late precolonial Polychrome Tradition research.

## Page 12

The paper compares documentary accounts of cultivation, fishing, and turtle management with the archaeological and environmental record, without treating the chronicles as transparent descriptions.

## Page 14

Regional archaeological data include large settlements, Polychrome ceramics, and exchange relationships across the Tefé and Coari portions of the Middle Solimões.
