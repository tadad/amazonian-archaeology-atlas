# Geography of Ancient Geometric Earthworks and Their Builders in Southwestern Amazonia

## Notes

Maps and classifies 1,279 earthworks over 27,569 square kilometres and reviews radiocarbon evidence for geoglyph and mound-village phases. The restricted local supplement is the authors' coordinate list used only to derive coarse public density cells.
