# As cerâmicas dos sítios a céu aberto de Monte Alegre

## Notes

Initial ceramic analysis from open-air sites around Monte Alegre. The repository summary describes occupations beginning around the twelfth century AD and a local ceramic pattern incorporating Santarém, Konduri, and Koriabo-related elements.

This late sequence is more directly relevant to the world encountered in 1542 than the much earlier Pedra Pintada deposits, while still representing regional comparison rather than a one-to-one identification with a chronicled settlement.
