# La arqueología del río Napo: noticias recientes y desafíos futuros

## Notes

Regional archaeological synthesis and new survey evidence for the Napo. It offers material context for the river corridor while warning against treating an ethnohistorical name as a direct archaeological identification.

## Page 4

The regional map locates investigated sites and communities along the middle and lower Napo, including Angoteros Pampa.

## Page 5

The paper summarizes Carvajal's itinerary and places the reported Lesser Aparia corridor between the Aguarico and Curaray mouths and farther down the middle/lower Napo.

## Page 11

Phase Napo ceramics are reported at Angoteros Pampa alongside evidence from other river communities and sites.

## Page 16

The conclusion says these finds may be among the first archaeological evidence relevant to Lesser Aparia, but it also presents broader regional continuity as an alternative explanation.

## Page 17

The authors emphasize that the available evidence does not directly prove that the investigated sites were the settlements named in the sixteenth-century account.
