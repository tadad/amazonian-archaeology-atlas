# A terra do ‘grande senhor Aparia’ e seus descendentes

## Notes

An archaeological, ethnohistorical, and ethnographic argument about the geography of Aparia and Omágua/Kambeba history. Its proposed locations are hypotheses, not settled identifications.

## Page 1

The abstract associates a Lesser Aparia center with the Napo–Curaray confluence and proposes that the principal Aparia center or zone of influence may have been at modern São Paulo de Olivença.

## Page 2

The paper explicitly frames São Paulo de Olivença as a hypothesis and states that no conclusive evidence presently fixes the capital or main expansion center.

## Page 3

The discussion describes archaeology near the Curaray and a 2022 reconnaissance from Letícia through São Paulo de Olivença and Santo Antônio do Içá.

## Page 6

The paper revisits the expedition's descriptions of dense Machiparo settlement and reports of gold, silver, and powerful communities, treating them as ethnohistorical evidence to be critically located.
