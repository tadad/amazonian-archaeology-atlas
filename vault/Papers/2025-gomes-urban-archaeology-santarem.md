# Urban Archaeology in the Lower Amazon

## Notes

Urban fieldwork defining the spatial organization and chronology of the Aldeia and Porto archaeological sites beneath modern Santarém. The publisher abstract reports areas of approximately 121 and 89 hectares and dates their contemporaneity to AD 1200–1600.

The article interprets both as habitation sites and identifies Aldeia as a likely ceremonial convergence center. This is a strong material comparison for the sixteenth-century accounts of large lower-Amazon settlements, but it does not independently verify every political claim in the chronicle.
