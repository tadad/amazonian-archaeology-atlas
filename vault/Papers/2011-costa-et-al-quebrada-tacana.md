# Mineralogy and chemistry of archaeological ceramic fragments from Quebrada Tacana

## Notes

Archaeometric study of ceramic fragments from the Quebrada Tacana archaeological-dark-earth site in Leticia County, Colombia. The study characterizes pottery fabrics, tempers, mineral phases, and chemical composition.

## Evidence reviewed

The site is located near Leticia and the Brazilian border. It occupies about two hectares of Late Holocene floodplain, with a ceramic-bearing anthropogenic horizon and charcoal dated to approximately 1270–1060 years BP.
