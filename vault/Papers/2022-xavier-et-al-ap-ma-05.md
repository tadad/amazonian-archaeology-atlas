# Aspectos morfológicos e texturais de antrossolos no AP-MA-05

## Notes

Study of anthropogenic soils at AP-MA-05 on the UNIFAP campus in Macapá. The site contains dense ceramic fragments and funerary urns; the authors compare 41 soil samples from the site with adjacent soil and discuss anthropogenic modification and conservation threats.
