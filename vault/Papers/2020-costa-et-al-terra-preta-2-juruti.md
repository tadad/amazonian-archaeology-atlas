# Geoarqueologia do Sítio Terra Preta 2

## Notes

Geoarchaeological study of anthropogenic dark earth at Terra Preta 2 in Juruti municipality. Ceramic material was recorded through 46 systematic probes, and soil morphology, chemistry, and stratigraphy were studied across the site.

The article reports an archaeological-dark-earth extent of about 28 hectares and spatial differences interpreted as evidence of movement and varied everyday activities during occupation.
