# Análise tecno-estilística do Sítio Pedra Chata

## Notes

Techno-stylistic analysis of ceramics excavated by the Baixo Urubu Project at Pedra Chata in Itacoatiara municipality. The repository record reports the oldest dated ceramic-producing context then known for the lower Urubu, approximately 200–50 BC, and a cultural sequence that may continue without a hiatus into the early colonial period.

## Caution

The atlas uses modern Itacoatiara as a public research anchor and does not publish the AM-IT-30 site coordinate.
