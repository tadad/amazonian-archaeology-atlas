# Estudos arqueológicos na região de Porto Trombetas, PA

## Notes

Regional synthesis of Pocó and Konduri ceramic occupations and later contract archaeology around Porto Trombetas. The PDF identifies the issue as volume 24(2), pages 50–73, with a 2012 imprint.

## Page 2

The abstract describes historical reports of populous settlements and hierarchical organization alongside archaeological evidence for Pocó and Konduri occupations.

## Page 5

The historical review describes dense lower-Amazon settlement from the lower Nhamundá eastward, providing a critical regional frame for the expedition narrative.

## Page 6

Following Porro, the authors outline a São João province from the lower Nhamundá through the Trombetas/Óbidos area toward the Tapajós and note the warrior-women tradition associated with the upper Nhamundá.

## Page 15

The Boa Vista investigations and other Porto Trombetas work document substantial riverine and interfluvial archaeological occupations; they do not prove a one-to-one match with Carvajal's named settlements.
