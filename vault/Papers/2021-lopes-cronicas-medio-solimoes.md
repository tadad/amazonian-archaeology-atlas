# Crônicas amazônicas e trocas indígenas

## Notes

Documentary archaeology of the Middle Solimões. The article compares colonial narratives with settlement and exchange evidence while cautioning against assigning a passage to a single site.

## Page 4

Using Porro's critical geography, the paper associates the Middle Solimões with the province called Machifaro/Machiparo in early narratives.

## Page 5

The regional map places archaeological research around the Caiambé and Tefé lake systems and the São João site within the broader Middle Solimões study area.

## Page 9

The São João site is described as an extensive riverine settlement near the Caiambé mouth, with archaeological evidence relevant to late precolonial regional occupation.

## Page 10

The author compares the archaeological pattern with chroniclers' descriptions but explicitly rejects a direct one-passage-to-one-site identification.
