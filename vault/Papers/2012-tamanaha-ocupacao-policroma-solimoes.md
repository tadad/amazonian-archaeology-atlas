# Ocupação polícroma no baixo e médio rio Solimões

## Notes

Master's dissertation on Guarita-phase occupations in the middle and lower Solimões. It analyzes chronology, ceramic assemblages, artifact density, and spatial distribution at sites in Coari municipality and near the Solimões–Negro confluence.

The repository abstract describes these as the latest Indigenous ceramic occupations in the study area, continuing into the sixteenth century. This makes the work directly relevant to evaluating late-precolonial settlement along the route without equating any excavated site with a named place in Carvajal.
