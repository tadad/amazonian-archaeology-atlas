# Sítios arqueológicos de ocupação sazonal na Costa Estuarina do Amapá

## Notes

Comparative study of seven low-density archaeological sites in metropolitan Macapá and the estuarine coast. The article draws on survey, excavation, spatial information, and ceramic collections produced by about a decade of regional work.

## Pages 120–151

The authors interpret the small and shallow sites as seasonal components of a broader settlement system that also included large villages and cemeteries. The study therefore adds settlement diversity to the downstream end of the route rather than implying that every location was a permanent town.
