# Taqrachullu

## Discovery summary

Taqrachullu, Pukara Taqrachullu, T'akrachullu, Pukara T'akrachullu (Quechua t'akra uncultivated land, an infertile or rocky piece of land, chullu spur of land which ends in the confluence of two rivers / something soaked) or María Fortaleza is an archaeological site in Peru. It is located in the Cusco Region, Espinar Province, Suykutambo District. The site was declared a National Cultural Heritage (Patrimonio Cultural) of Peru.
