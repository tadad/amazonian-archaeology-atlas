# Cojitambo

## Discovery summary

Cojitambo is an Inca and pre-Inca archaeological ruin, a popular rock climbing site, and a small village west of Azogues, capital of Canar province of Ecuador. The name is said by the Ecuadorian Ministry of Tourism to derive from the Quechua language curi tambo, or Inn of Gold (although no gold has been found at the site).
