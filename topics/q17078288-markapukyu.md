# Markapukyu

## Discovery summary

Markapukyu (Hispanicized spelling Marcapuquio) is an archaeological site in Peru. It is situated in the Pasco Region, Pasco Province, Yarusyacán District, at a height of 3,787 metres (12,425 ft). The name is derived from Quechua marka, meaning 'village' or 'storey', and pukyu, meaning 'spring of water',
