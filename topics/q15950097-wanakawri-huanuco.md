# Wanakawri, Huánuco

## Discovery summary

Wanakawri (Quechua, Hispanicized and mixed spellings Huanacaure, Wanacaure) is an archaeological site in Peru. It is situated in the Huánuco Region, Huánuco Province, Chinchao District, southeast of San Pablo de Pillao, at a height of about 3,359 metres (11,020 ft).
